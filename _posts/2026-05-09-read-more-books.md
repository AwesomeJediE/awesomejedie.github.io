a year ago i determined i wanted to read more books, whether physically holding it or listening to an audio book. since last may, these are all of the books i finished 

- Ready Player One
- The Total Money Makeover
- Warriors: Lost Stars
- The Gay Agenda
- We Have Always Been Here
- A Short History of Queer Women
- Pride: The Story of Harvey Milk and the Rainbow Flag
- Lesbian Couples: A Guide to Creating Healthy Relationships
- Messy Roots: A Graphic Memoir of a Wuhanese American
- Kirby's Lessons For Falling (in Love)
- The Magic Fish
- Hijab Butch Blues
- Thank You For Calling The Lesbian Line
- Moby Dyke: An Obsessive Quest To Track Down The Last Remaining Lesbian Bars in America

- The Secrets and Mysteries of Hawaii: A Call To The Soul
- The Best We Could Do
- White Fragility: Why It's So Hard For White People To Talk About Racism
- Aloha Betrayed
- Everything in Color

there’s a lot of other books i read but didn’t finish, but 20 finished books in a year is pretty rad 

**favorite book:** Hijab Butch Blues was an incredible memoir and even though I finished it in August i still think about it often. there were many fantastic memoirs, books, & graphic novels overall, but that is the one that stood out to me the most. Lamya is a very poetic and moving writer and i highly recommend the book

**least favorite book:** generally if i hate a book i wont finish it. but i pushed myself though The Secrets and Mysteries of Hawaii due to buying it used & it being related to Miracle Musical. The author uses weird racist stereotypes and has creepy statements about women but between my tears i can laugh at his insistence of trademarking the work GodLink™️ in his erratic mythology of the world 

perhaps this next year i can finish more than 20 books teehee….
